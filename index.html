Why outcome-based?

TAKE today rewards villages for meeting indicator checklists. The fires of 2026 concentrated in districts that already receive ecological awards. BASAH keeps the existing TAKE base but turns part of it into a bonus paid only for measured wet peat, less burned area and fewer hotspots.

Scoring rule

Score (0–100) = 50 × G + 35 × B + 15 × H G = share of dry-season days with water table ≤ 40 cm below surface B = 1 − 0.5 × (burned / own 3-yr average), floored at 0 H = max(0, 1 − hotspots / 10) Base = pool × (1 − bonus share) ÷ number of villages Bonus = pool × bonus share × (score_i ÷ Σ eligible scores) Eligible only if burned ≤ own 3-yr average (else bonus withheld, base is always protected)
Weights are a pilot design proposed in the essay (Appendix C) and are meant to be set by regent regulation after consultation.
